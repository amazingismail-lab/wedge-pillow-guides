Vekkia vs Bueoure Wedge Pillow: Which Height Fits You?

By James Park · September 2026

Anyone comparing vekkia vs bueoure wedge pillows is really comparing two heights on the same footprint. Both wedges measure 31 by 24 inches according to their maker listings, so the choice comes down to a 7.5-inch rise (Vekkia) versus a 5.5-inch rise (Bueoure), plus a few differences in cover and construction. How different are the two heights, really?

Two inches sounds small, but over the same 31-inch length it changes the slope by roughly a third. Dividing height by length gives about 14 degrees for the Vekkia and about 10 degrees for the Bueoure, assuming the incline runs straight along the full length. Neither maker states an angle outright, so this is arithmetic from their own dimensions, the same approach used in this site's wedge pillow height guide.

Note

Bueoure's title says "Perfect Incline Angle," but the listing never gives a number, and one gallery image labels the sloped surface as 32 inches against stated dimensions of 31. That's most likely the diagonal length of the slope, not a second size.

What do the covers and extras add?

Vekkia lists more hardware: a zip-off Dutch velvet cover, two side pockets, a carry handle, and a stated weight of 3.7 pounds. Bueoure's listing leans on construction instead, describing one-piece cut foam with no adhesive, a breathable cover with an extra-long zipper, and side pockets that appear in its details table and gallery but not in its bullet points. Both covers are machine washable, though neither listing gives a wash temperature.

Who might get more out of the taller Vekkia?

Sleepers who want more lift from a single wedge, rather than stacking a pillow on top, may prefer the Vekkia's 7.5-inch rise. That extra height and steeper slope is closer to what's used in reflux research, though no study has tested this specific pillow. If a taller incline ends up pushing the head too far forward or straining the neck, that's worth reconsidering rather than pushing through it.

Who might prefer the gentler Bueoure?

Sleepers who find steep wedges uncomfortable, or who mainly want a mild raise for reading or snoring-related comfort, may lean toward the Bueoure's 5.5-inch height. Its maker markets the shorter rise as designed to pair with your own pillow, a combination covered in this site's guide to using a pillow on top of a wedge.

Can either pillow actually help with reflux or snoring?

Elevating the head of the bed may help some people with night-time reflux, but the evidence is limited and neither wedge has been studied directly. The American College of Gastroenterology's 2022 guideline treats head-of-bed elevation as a conditional recommendation based on low-quality evidence, and a 2021 review of five trials in 228 patients found symptom improvement reported in four of them. Anyone using a wedge to manage reflux, snoring, or sleep apnea symptoms that persist should talk to a doctor rather than rely on elevation alone.

What don't the listings tell you?

Neither page states a foam density, an indentation firmness rating, a warranty, or a certification such as CertiPUR-US, which checks foam for formaldehyde, ozone depleters, heavy metals, and low VOC emissions. Bueoure's title also reads "FSA Eligible" without explaining why.

Watch out

Under IRS Publication 502, an item must be primarily to treat or prevent illness — not just generally beneficial — to qualify for FSA reimbursement. Confirm eligibility with your FSA administrator before buying on the strength of that label.

Frequently asked questions

What's the main difference between the Vekkia and Bueoure wedge pillows?

Height. Both measure 31 by 24 inches per their listings, but the Vekkia rises 7.5 inches and the Bueoure 5.5 inches. That works out to roughly a 14-degree slope versus a 10-degree slope over the same length, so the Vekkia sits noticeably higher and steeper than the Bueoure.

Is a steeper wedge better for acid reflux?

Not necessarily — research on wedge elevation for reflux is limited and hasn't tested either specific product. Guidelines describe head-of-bed elevation as a conditional recommendation based on low-quality evidence, so a taller wedge isn't guaranteed to work better than a shorter one, and comfort matters too.

Can I wash the covers on these wedge pillows?

Yes, both listings describe removable, machine-washable covers — Vekkia's zips off and Bueoure's uses an extra-long zipper. Neither maker states a wash temperature or drying method, so following the gentlest reasonable setting is the safer default until more detail is available.

Do either of these wedges have a foam certification?

Neither listing mentions one. Certifications like CertiPUR-US check foam for substances such as formaldehyde and heavy metals and for low chemical emissions; without that label stated, buyers can't confirm those standards from the listing alone.

How long do these pillows take to expand after shipping?

Both arrive compressed. Vekkia's listing recommends waiting up to 48 hours before use, while Bueoure's gallery suggests about 24 hours for the foam to reach full shape and firmness.
